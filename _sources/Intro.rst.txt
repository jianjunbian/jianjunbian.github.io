---------

Crystal Dislocation Generator (CryDisGen) is a versatile scientific software toolkit designed to facilitate the construction of realistic dislocation structures in crystalline materials for molecular dynamics (MD) simulations. Developed by researchers J. Bian, W. Yuan et al., CryDisGen enables the creation of atomic-scale models featuring arbitrary dislocation configurations in cubic, such as face-centered cubic (FCC) and body-centered cubic (BCC), and hexagonal close-packed (HCP) crystal structures. 

In contrast to existing toolkits, which are often limited to generating dislocations with relatively simple geometries, CryDisGen employs the displacement field derived from the classical Burgers model to generate dislocation structures with a wide range of geometries. This capability facilitates the construction of complex three-dimensional dislocation configurations at the atomic scale and provides an alternative approach to existing dislocation construction methods.

Implemented in object-oriented C++ and compatible with popular MD simulation package such as LAMMPS, CryDisGen provides a flexible and user-friendly framework for generating customized dislocation models. By facilitating the study of dislocation-related plasticity and mechanical behavior at the atomic scale, CryDisGen serves as an advanced toolkit in the field of atomic modelling and enhances the generalizability of atomic simulations for crystalline materials.
